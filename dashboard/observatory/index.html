Data sources, limitations and removal requests

This independent, non-commercial educational project uses exclusively open-licensed public datasets: OpenStreetMap transit relations and road hierarchy (ODbL 1.0) and Davao City barangay boundaries sourced from NAMRIA and PSA via OCHA COD-AB through faeldon/philippines-json-maps (CC BY-IGO).

LTFRB-derived route records, raw government files, private source identifiers and review history are excluded from this public release. Structural checks do not confirm active transit services, official route identity, boarding access or transfers.

We review correction or removal requests promptly. Request a correction or removal without posting personal or confidential information.
