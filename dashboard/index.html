Four evidence-based findings

Source: osm_davao_transit_elements_20260708.json. Counts describe the processed OSM extract, not officially authorized Davao service.

FIGURE 01

Route sequencing

Only 7 of 14 route relations have ordered stop members. This is an OSM mapping gap, not evidence a service is inactive.

FIGURE 02

Metadata quality

52 stops have no name; 4 lack usable point coordinates. Both limit offline route search.

FIGURE 03

Coordinate spread

Some retained relation members lie outside Davao City. Geographic scope must be verified before making local coverage claims.

FIGURE 04

Candidate interchanges

10 stop identifiers are shared by multiple routes. They are not verified usable transfers.

Cautious inference

These records reveal a coverage and validation problem, not a finished multimodal transport network. Even though 30 adjacent stop pairs can be derived, no valid basis exists here for estimating actual fare, passenger wait, service status, or fastest door-to-door route. Many retained stop records are associated with nonlocal intercity routes.

Recommended next step: verify local route geography and ordered boarding locations, then integrate dated official fares, schedules, pedestrian network and any authorized tricycle zones. Treat shared stop IDs as unverified transfer candidates.
